# ``KlaviyoLocation``

Trigger Klaviyo geofence events from the user's location.

## Overview

`KlaviyoLocation` adds geofencing to `KlaviyoSDK`. Initialize `KlaviyoSwift` first, then
register for geofencing to monitor the geofences configured in your Klaviyo account.
