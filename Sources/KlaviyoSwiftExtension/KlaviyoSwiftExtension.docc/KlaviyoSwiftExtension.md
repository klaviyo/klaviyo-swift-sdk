# ``KlaviyoSwiftExtension``

Add rich media and badge counts to Klaviyo push notifications.

## Overview

`KlaviyoSwiftExtension` runs inside your app's Notification Service Extension. Forward the
extension's callbacks to ``KlaviyoExtensionSDK`` to download rich media attachments and update
the badge count.
